# Gabriella: design brief

## Product
IELTS vocabulary and practice site for adult learners (18 to 35), mostly on phones.
Pages: Home, Words (topic word grid with Know/Unsure/Don't know marks),
Flashcards, Quiz, Speaking (cue card + 1:00 prep and 2:00 talk timers),
Writing (Task 2 prompt, 40:00 timer, word count).

## Feeling
Calm, focused, encouraging. Like a well-made paper notebook, not a SaaS dashboard.

## Colour
- Base: white #FFFFFF. At least 85% of every screen is white.
- Accent: pink #D6336C. Only for brand, primary buttons, active states, focus.
- Ink #1F1A24, muted #6B6570, hairlines #EDE6EA.
- Status colours must NOT be pink: Know green #2F8F5B, Unsure amber #C98410,
  Don't know grey #6B6570.

## Type
- UI and headings: Manrope (800 for headings, 600 for labels).
- Definitions and example sentences: Literata, example sentences in italic.

## Rules
- Simple by design: one accent, generous white space, few borders, no gradients.
- No card grids of identical boxes, no all-caps labels, no stock illustrations.
- Mobile first. Large tap targets for flashcards and quiz answers.

## Avoid
Generic ed-tech look: purple gradients, mascots, confetti, dashboard widgets.
